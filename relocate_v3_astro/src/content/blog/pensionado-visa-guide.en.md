**Panama's Pensionado Visa grants permanent residency to retirees with a verified lifetime pension of at least $1,000/month** — and comes with some of the best retiree discounts in the world, from 50% off entertainment to 25% off airfare. Here's exactly what it requires, what it costs, and how the process works.

## What Is the Pensionado Visa?

The Pensionado Visa is Panama's dedicated permanent residency program for retirees. Unlike programs that require a large upfront investment, it's built around a single core requirement: a verified pension of at least $1,000/month, guaranteed for life. Once approved, it grants permanent residency status immediately — not a temporary or provisional status that needs periodic renewal like some other countries' retirement visas.

What sets it apart internationally is the combination of permanent residency plus Panama's long-standing "Jubilado" retiree discount program, which applies once you hold the visa. Very few countries pair a straightforward, accessible residency threshold with this level of everyday discounts on entertainment, travel, dining, and healthcare — which is a major reason Panama consistently ranks among the top retirement destinations for Americans, Canadians, and Europeans.

## Requirements Checklist

**Minimum pension of $1,000/month.** This is the central requirement. The pension must be lifetime and verifiable — a one-time payout or a pension with an end date generally will not qualify.

**Certified proof of pension income.** You'll need an official, certified letter from the pension provider (government agency, employer pension fund, or private annuity provider) confirming the monthly amount and that it is guaranteed for life.

**Valid passport with 6+ months validity.** Your passport must remain valid well beyond your planned application and processing timeline.

**Police clearance from your country of origin.** A background check showing no criminal record, typically required from your home country and sometimes your current country of residence if different.

**Health certificate.** A basic medical certificate confirming you don't have a condition that would be considered a public health risk.

## The Retiree Discounts (Panama's Jubilado Program)

Once you hold the Pensionado Visa, you gain access to Panama's retiree discount program, commonly referred to as the "Jubilado" benefits. These include:

- **50% off entertainment and hotels** — movies, concerts, sporting events, and hotel stays.
- **25% off airline tickets** — on many routes with participating carriers.
- **25% off restaurants** — at many establishments across the country.
- **15% off hospital bills** — for medical services at participating facilities.
- **Tax exemption on the import of household goods** — a one-time benefit when you first relocate your belongings to Panama.

These are part of Panama's broader retiree discount program; our team will confirm the exact current terms and participating providers as part of your consultation, since specific percentages and eligible vendors can be updated by the government.
